# Calorik Design Context

## Users
Casual health-conscious people who log meals when they remember, mainly watching calories without obsessing over every macro. They check the app a few times a day — quick in, quick out. Not fitness bros, not dietitians. Normal people who want a gentle nudge to eat better.

## Brand Personality
**Clean, confident, effortless** — like Things 3 or Apple Health. The app should feel like it respects your time and intelligence. No hand-holding, no gamification, no guilt. Quiet competence.

## Aesthetic Direction
**Refined minimal.** Let iOS 26 Liquid Glass breathe — use it sparingly and purposefully, not on every surface. The content IS the design. Editorial typography, generous whitespace, clear hierarchy. The app should feel like it barely tries, yet everything is in the right place.

- System adaptive tinting (no hardcoded brand color — let the wallpaper set the mood)
- Glass reserved for interactive containers and navigation — not every surface
- Food entries and daily progress are the hero, not chrome or decoration
- Left-aligned where possible, centered only when it serves the content

## Anti-References
- **MyFitnessPal**: Cluttered, ad-filled, overwhelming feature creep
- **Overly gamified apps** (Duolingo-style): No badges, streaks, confetti, achievement popups
- **Generic AI dashboards**: No purple gradients, hero metric templates, glowing accent cards
- **Clinical health apps**: No sterile white, hospital vibes

## Design Principles
1. **Less glass, more air** — Reserve Liquid Glass for containers that need it (cards, nav). Let whitespace and typography do the heavy lifting.
2. **The food list is the product** — The meal log should be reachable and prominent. The gauge and macros support it, not the other way around.
3. **One tap, done** — Primary actions (add entry, analyze photo) must be obvious and fast. No hunting.
4. **Quiet confidence** — No exclamation marks, no celebration animations, no motivational copy. Just clear, calm information.
5. **Earn every element** — If it doesn't help the user log food or check progress, remove it.
